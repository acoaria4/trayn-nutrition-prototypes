# TRAYN v3 — live Shopify SEO audit

Audited 23 October 2026 (Asia/Kolkata). Target: [traynnutrition.com](https://traynnutrition.com/).

**Verdict: the site is accessible to crawlers, but the product pages and shared SEO setup need foundational work before an organic growth campaign.** The highest-value change is to publish complete, approved product information on the product URLs themselves, then use that information in metadata and structured data.

This was a read-only audit. No Shopify theme, product, policy, price, inventory, or checkout setting was changed. The live page identifies the published theme as **TRAYN V3 — Silver Identity**, theme ID `192621379872`, role `main`; this is evidence from the deployed site, not an assumption based on a local v3 folder.

## Scope and method

Applied the [Claude SEO full-audit workflow](https://github.com/AgriciDaniel/claude-seo/blob/main/skills/seo-audit/SKILL.md), its technical/content/schema/ecommerce checklists, and its evidence → dependencies → verification approach. These instructions were adapted to Codex's available HTTP and browser tools; the Claude plugin installer and bundled Python runtime were not installed or executed. Two specialist review passes covered content/ecommerce and technical/schema/sitemaps. Local v1/v2 source was used only to locate the store, never as proof of what is live.

Fetched robots.txt, the sitemap index and its five children, all listed public content URLs, the linked privacy policy, and three additional standard policy URLs. Nine HTML pages returned 200: homepage, four product pages, two collections, Contact and Privacy. The sitemap-listed blog returned 404. Agent discovery Markdown was also fetched. Private/transactional paths were excluded. There was no purchase, cart mutation or form submission.

Compared raw HTML with the rendered homepage and Belgian Chocolate product page. Reviewed desktop and a 390 × 844 phone viewport, including opening/closing the mobile menu. This is a small-store crawl of the discovered sitemap and navigation, not a guarantee that every unlinked URL has been found.

## Findings, in implementation order

### 1. High — Establish an approved retail product record

The [homepage's product section](https://traynnutrition.com/#product) gives detailed label values and a 500 g pouch, but explicitly says **“Packaging artwork, not an approved retail specification.”** It also displays a ₹2,999 purchase offer. The product page does not give the buyer a complete specification to resolve that uncertainty.

This is an observed trust/content conflict, not proof that the real SKU is mislabeled or that a law has been broken. The older local blend concept is not evidence that today's public WPC identity is wrong.

**Next action:** Confirm the actual formula, net quantity, serving information, approved nutrition/ingredients/allergens, current SKU/price and final label/pack images. Publish the same approved facts across the homepage, product page, label PDF, Shopify product record and commerce data. Keep any remaining concept disclosure accurate; do not simply erase it to imply approval.

**Dependency:** Product owner supplies or signs off the facts and supporting assets. **Acceptance:** One approved SKU record agrees across those surfaces. If approval remains unresolved, this action is incomplete even if copy has been rewritten.

### 2. High — Rebuild the product page's information, using that record

The [Belgian Chocolate product page](https://traynnutrition.com/products/trayn-whey-rich-chocolate) shows its title, price, quantity selector, purchase button and tax/shipping note. It does not present pack size, ingredients, nutrition, directions, product explanation or a useful product gallery. The homepage's “View product details” link therefore leads to less information. This was verified in both raw HTML and the live browser.

The Vanilla, Cafe Mocha and Cookies & Cream product pages are similarly sparse; they showed ₹3,299 and Sold Out during the crawl. They are still public and sitemap-listed. Do not automatically delete or noindex them: first distinguish real temporarily unavailable products from unfinished development entries.

**Next action:** Populate approved product-specific content and the shared product template. Include real front/back pack images, essential specifications, usage/allergen details, support and concise delivery/return information. Keep the established product handles. Include genuine reviews when available; never invent stars or testimonials.

**Dependency:** Finding 1 and an availability decision for each flavour. **Acceptance:** A direct search visitor can understand exactly what is offered without returning to the homepage; all four pages have accurate flavour-specific substance.

### 3. High — Repair Product structured data

All four product pages already emit validly parsed Product JSON-LD with name, brand and Offer price/currency/availability. **All four omit image and emit an empty description.** The chocolate Offer observed is INR 2999.00 / InStock; the other three are INR 3299.00 / OutOfStock. Those machine values agree with the observed page states; no inventory accuracy claim beyond that is made.

Product.image is required for Google's merchant-listing markup, so the missing field is a material eligibility gap. Description and useful, truthful product identifiers/policy information are further opportunities. This is a field-level assessment against [Google's merchant-listing documentation](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/appearance/structured-data/merchant-listing), not a Rich Results Test result or a Merchant Center diagnosis. Relative @id values and the http schema.org context are not treated as invalid JSON-LD.

**Next action:** Connect approved images and descriptions to the actual Shopify products; ensure the theme emits them along with real dynamic offers. Add Organization/WebSite identity on the homepage and breadcrumbs where useful. Do not add fake GTINs, ratings, licences or certifications.

**Dependency:** Findings 1–2. **Acceptance:** Required-field errors clear in Rich Results Test and rendered/raw data matches the page; Merchant Center checks are separate. Rich-result display is never guaranteed.

### 4. High — Add page-specific search metadata

All nine successful HTML pages lack meta descriptions. All also lack og:title, og:description and og:image; only og:site_name is present. The homepage title is simply “TRAYN Nutrition”; its H1 is a brand slogan. Product titles distinguish flavours but do not communicate the approved pack size/formulation.

**Next action:** Add shared theme support for page descriptions and sharing tags, then write unique, factual page values. Make the homepage title and an accompanying visible sentence explain the actual whey product/category while preserving the campaign headline. Improve the generic “Home page” collection title if that collection has a continuing purpose.

**Dependency:** Approved identity/product facts. **Acceptance:** Each intended landing page emits one appropriate title/description plus a working image preview. A missing description is not an indexing block or direct ranking penalty; Google can generate or rewrite snippets. See [Google's title guidance](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/appearance/title-link) and [snippet guidance](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/appearance/snippet).

### 5. High — Publish and expose buying policies

Only [Privacy policy](https://traynnutrition.com/policies/privacy-policy) is linked in the audited footer. The checked `/policies/shipping-policy`, `/policies/refund-policy` and `/policies/terms-of-service` each return 404. No equivalent buying-policy links were discovered in the audited public navigation. This does not prove that no draft policy exists in Admin.

**Next action:** Confirm actual shipping regions, charges/timing, returns/refunds/cancellation terms and business terms; publish them through Shopify and link them in the footer and near buying information. Keep any feed/schema policy data consistent.

**Dependency:** Owner-approved commercial terms. **Acceptance:** The links return useful policy content; product summaries, policies and checkout settings agree. These are trust improvements, not a claim that this audit established a specific legal or platform violation.

### 6. Medium — Fill Contact and make company identity easier to verify

The [Contact page](https://traynnutrition.com/pages/contact) has a heading and an empty main content body. The global footer provides email. A phone number and postal address do exist in the privacy policy, so the finding is poor discovery, not complete absence of business identity.

**Next action:** Publish validated support/business details on Contact and link to it. Expand the brand story with real people/processes and supporting product evidence where available. Attribute future educational supplement advice to appropriate qualified reviewers; no decorative credentials or invented testing claims.

**Dependency:** Confirmation of the public support information/evidence. **Acceptance:** Buyers can find a usable support route and business identity without reading the privacy policy.

### 7. Medium — Connect collections and the full catalogue

The two collections and Contact are in the sitemap but have no incoming links from other sampled HTML pages. Shop navigation points to the homepage purchase section. The other three flavours are discoverable through the collection/sitemap, but not a normal homepage category link. Product pages have no category breadcrumb or related-product route.

**Next action:** If the four-flavour catalogue is intentional, add a clear collection link and useful product/category navigation. Give the whey collection a concise factual introduction; decide whether the generic frontpage collection serves a separate purpose. Avoid mass-producing thin keyword pages.

**Dependency:** Catalogue/availability decision. **Acceptance:** Important pages have normal visible links and are reachable within a few clicks; re-crawl shows the intended link graph. Sitemap discovery means these are not proven completely undiscoverable URLs.

### 8. Medium — Resolve the sitemap's 404 blog

The working blog sitemap lists [blogs/news](https://traynnutrition.com/blogs/news), which returns a true HTTP 404. No useful articles were discovered in the sitemap crawl.

**Next action:** Reconcile the native Shopify blog/channel/template record. If publishing a blog, restore a useful 200 index; if unused, remove its advertisement through the platform's source configuration. Do not redirect an unrelated missing blog to the homepage merely to suppress a warning.

**Dependency:** Decision whether the blog is intended. **Acceptance:** Re-fetching every sitemap-listed public content URL yields the intended indexable content, with no advertised 404. One stale URL is not evidence that the whole store cannot be indexed.

### 9. Medium — Replace product-image placeholders

The chocolate product page uses an abstract chocolate illustration rather than the actual pouch. The other product pages use logo placeholder imagery. Homepage pouch images are explicitly concepts. Descriptive alts and WebP delivery are already useful strengths.

**Next action:** Add approved actual product/label photography or accurate final renders to each Shopify product, and use those in the gallery, Product.image and sharing cards. Consider responsive candidates for the homepage pouch, which served a 1024 px asset without srcset at an observed roughly 288 px display width. Treat that as an optimization opportunity, not measured evidence of a speed failure.

**Dependency:** Final assets for each actual product. **Acceptance:** Every SKU has appropriate gallery images, accurate alternative text and a fetchable schema/share image. Asset sizes and any speed benefit should be measured after implementation.

## Mobile, performance and AI-search assessment

One low-priority title-encoding defect was also found: the Cookies & Cream raw title double-escapes its ampersand, so the title contains a literal `&amp;` after HTML parsing. Fix the extra escaping while updating the shared title handling; verify the visible browser title and raw HTML, without changing the product handle.

The checked homepage and chocolate product page fit the phone viewport; document width did not exceed the layout viewport. The menu opened/closed and visible purchase controls were usable in this limited review. This is not a full accessibility certification, device matrix, or tested checkout flow. The product content gap persists on mobile and desktop.

The homepage uses high-priority hero images and lazy loads several later images. Main content is present in initial HTML; there is no observed blank-JavaScript-app indexing dependency. A hidden/lazy gallery image with no loaded natural dimensions at capture time is not automatically a broken image.

Both mobile and desktop PageSpeed Insights requests returned **429: daily quota exceeded**. LCP, INP, CLS, Lighthouse scores, real-user percentiles and a Lighthouse Agentic Browsing fraction are **not measured**. Normal response times in the HTTP crawl are not substitutes for Core Web Vitals.

The robots wildcard group allows public content, and agents.md plus page-provided Shopify WebMCP tools are present. No blanket AI-search bot block was observed; robots permission does not prove successful indexing or AI citation. No shopping tools were invoked. The useful nutrition table/FAQ on the homepage supports readable extraction, but weak product detail and incomplete brand/product records limit reliable answers. Focus on the same approved facts, crawlable pages and trust work above. Do not prioritize speculative AI-only files or promise citations. Backlinks, domain authority, keyword volumes/rankings, AI mentions, organic revenue, GSC indexing and Merchant Center approvals were not measured.

## Recommended next run

First settle approved product facts/assets and policies; then implement the product template, metadata, schema and navigation in an unpublished Shopify theme. Validate representative product states and the live/preview differences before publishing. Repair the stale blog sitemap source and fill Contact. Measure mobile/desktop performance when a working test quota is available, then inspect Search Console/Merchant Center with account access if provided.

Track deploy-time indicators first: four complete product pages, zero missing required Product.image fields, descriptions on intended landing pages, working policies, and no advertised sitemap 404. After recrawling, monitor relevant Search Console impressions/clicks, product enhancement reports and landing-page conversion; no uplift is guaranteed. The next audit should check these same URLs against this baseline and clearly separate deployed fixes from unpublished changes.
