# V4 — The Work

Approved 1 November 2026: independent prototype, cinematic training × product craft, real Blender/Three.js product experience. No Shopify migration or publication is authorized.

## Visual score

Palette: foil #090909, graphite #191a1b, silver #f0f0ec, muted #a2a29e, packaging red #f82030, warm cocoa #442a22. Broad Manrope display typography carries the opening; condensed Barlow carries the GET BUILT poster. Monospaced captions describe actual content and controls.

Hero: full-bleed training photography with broad readable typography; a native-sticky 2,040 px sequence moves through the work into a physically lit pouch. The same Three.js asset becomes a touch/keyboard-accessible inspection scene later. Native scrolling, measured persistent header, stable phone viewport geometry.

GET BUILT: a red editorial spread, training imagery revealed through a changing aperture, large typography crossing the image edge, quiet stationary reading copy. Flavour: cinematic chocolate across an asymmetric black composition. Packaging: close-up into the real 3D model, then direct manipulation. Product: calm silver/charcoal label reader; no invented commerce claims. Questions: responsive native disclosures. Closing: a quiet locally rendered product scene.

Content order remains hero 01/02 → mindset 03 → packaging 04 → details 05 → questions 06 → contact. The flavour image belongs to the hero exit/mindset transition. No additional long pins, forced section heights on phones, scroll trapping or autoplay audio.

## Media allocation

Higgsfield: six 2K Cinema Studio Image 2.5 stills at 2 credits each; two four-second 720p Cinema Studio Video 3.0 training films at 20 credits each. Up to four replacement stills at 2 credits each. Expected 52; hard cap 60. Initial style images are production assets, not extra jobs. Recheck prices before each submission; no extra video attempt without permission. Persist every job before polling; never retry uncertain submissions.

Blender: proportion-based pouch reconstruction from the supplied front/back references, not a manufacturing specification; UV-mapped original artwork, foil material and physical edge construction. Product/detail/closing compositions in landscape and portrait are rendered locally. Local motion exports reuse these approved images and geometry.

The supplied printer PDF's back-page transcription is retained as artwork information, distinct from verified retail records. No invented prices, inventory, claims, endorsements or certificates. Shop links go to the existing store; this prototype cannot place orders.

## Build milestones

1. Storyboard and colour/type tokens; independent V4 package and credit ledger.
2. Blender mesh/material proof, GLB export and browser inspection.
3. Desktop/phone campaign compositions and complete hero handoff.
4. All chapters, inspection, reading controls, disclosures and closing.
5. Responsive, WebKit, reduced-motion, no-JS, WebGL fallback, keyboard, context-loss and prefix-hosting verification. Compare preserved-version fingerprints.

Targets: one active renderer; visibility/demand-driven drawing; bounded device pixel ratio; GLB and textures near 2 MB; fewer than 40k triangles; progressive below-fold media; only one decorative film playing. First-use and warm performance are checked independently. Physical phone performance cannot be established from emulation alone.
