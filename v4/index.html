Your daily ritual.

Preparation instructions as printed

  1. Measure.

    Mix 1 scoop (approx. 33 g) with 200 ml cold water or milk in a glass or shaker cup.

  2. Mix.

    Stir or shake until well mixed.

  3. Store.

    Store in a cool & dry place. Protect from direct sunlight. Store out of reach of children.

Usage and safety notes +

The artwork recommends two servings daily and states not to exceed the recommended daily usage. It is not recommended for children and is not for medicinal use.

Health supplements are not a substitute for a varied diet. The label states that this product is not intended to diagnose, treat, cure or prevent any disease. Pregnant or lactating women, or people with known medical conditions or taking medication, should consult a medical practitioner before consuming it.
