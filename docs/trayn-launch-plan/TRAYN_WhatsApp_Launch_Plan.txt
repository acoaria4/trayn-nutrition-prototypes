Hi, here's the launch direction for TRAYN.

*USP: Chosen to a standard. Tested before release.*
Our commitment is to select ingredients against clear quality standards, independently test manufactured batches before sale, and make relevant batch results accessible. We'll finalise and document the ingredient standards, testing and release process before making public claims.

*Pre-launch | 4-6 weeks before launch*
• Finalise the product, pricing, ingredient standards, testing, release criteria and delivery.
• Build a waitlist and share our founder story, ingredient choices and testing approach.
• Prepare the website and sampling programme; gather blind taste and mixability feedback on released product.
• Open sales only after the manufactured batch meets our agreed release criteria and ordering/fulfilment are ready.

*Launch and post-launch | Days 0-30*
• Launch to the waitlist, then our wider audience, with the relevant test report available.
• Start small with sampling and paid marketing, within an agreed budget.
• Track purchases and deliveries; gather honest feedback on taste, mixability, packaging and value.
• Fix customer issues and improve the website using real questions and reviews.

*Days 31-60*
• Improve the messages and channels that bring suitable customers.
• Introduce replenishment reminders based on actual usage and continue productive gym/creator partnerships.
• Track repeat purchases, acquisition costs and contribution per order.

*Days 61-90*
• Review sales, repeat buying, feedback, marketing costs and remaining stock.
• Increase spend only where the numbers and operations support it.
• Plan the next batch; consider new flavours only where demand supports them.

Please turn this into a weekly calendar with owners, costs and measurable targets. Let's agree the launch date, available stock and budget before setting sales targets.
